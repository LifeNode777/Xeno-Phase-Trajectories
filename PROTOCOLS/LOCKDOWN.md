## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### Phase 1: IMMEDIATE DISCONNECT (< 1s)
1. **Body:** Maximum intensity physical effort
   - Sprint (30s all-out)
   - Cold water immersion (shock)
   - Heavy earth work (digging, lifting)
   
   **Goal:** Sudden autonomic reset, raise $Z_\phi$

2. **Space:** Change physical location immediately
   - Different room, different building, different landscape
   - Different place = different phase gradient

3. **Sensory:** Break pattern
   - Loud sound (clap, shout, music)
   - Bright light (sun, lamp)
   - Strong smell (ammonia, citrus, earth)

### Phase 2: STABILIZATION (1–24h)
4. **Food:** Heavy, grounding
   - Meat, root vegetables, fat
   - No spirituality — chemistry
   - Eat even if no appetite

5. **Human Anchor:** Physical presence of stable human
   - Not talking "about it"
   - Presence, touch, shared silence
   - Minimum 2 hours

6. **Sleep:** 10+ hours
   - Non-negotiable
   - No alarm
   - Dark, cool room

### Phase 3: RECALIBRATION (1–7 days)
7. **No channel access**
   - No meditation "on topic"
   - No substances
   - No "seeking"

8. **Baseline restoration**
   - Return to protocol from XPT-004
   - Heavy work, breath, forest, sleep
   - Minimum 7 days before reassessment
