# PROTOCOL: Λ-REENTRY (LAMBDA-REENTRY)

**STATUS:** Operational  
**CLASSIFICATION:** Recovery  
**TRIGGER:** Post-lockdown, post-decoherence, post-trauma  
**TIME:** 7–30 days

---

## ABSTRACT

Topological rebinding protocol.

After LOCKDOWN or spontaneous decoherence, system must rebind to new attractor, not old one.

Old geometry collapsed. New one must crystallize.

---

## PHASES

### Phase 1: META FREEZE (Days 1–3)
**Action:** Stop all Floquet drives

- No meditation
- No substances
- No "seeking"
- No intense stimuli

**Goal:** Let old attractor fully dissolve. Don't interfere.

**Body:**
- Sleep as much as needed
- Eat simple, grounding food
- Minimal movement (walking only)

**Mind:**
- Accept confusion
- Don't force clarity
- Let thoughts flow without engagement

### Phase 2: BIOS ANCHORING (Days 4–10)
**Action:** Return to raw, unprocessed BIOS rhythm

**Body:**
- Heavy physical work (wood, earth, water)
- Hands in soil (no gloves)
- Cold exposure (air, water)
- Sleep with sunset, wake with sunrise

**Breath:**
- Natural, uncontrolled
- Observe, don't modify
- Let body find its rhythm

**Food:**
- Fermented (kimchi, sauerkraut, kefir)
- Root vegetables
- Meat with bone (if omnivore)
- Zero ultra-processed

**Goal:** Re-anchor in biological baseline band (BPB).

### Phase 3: TOPOLOGICAL REBINDING (Days 11–21)
**Action:** New geometry crystallizes

**Signs of rebinding:**
- $\theta$ stabilizes ≥ 0.65
- Body warm, breath deep, gut calm
- Spontaneous joy (not forced)
- Relations stabilize

**Practice:**
- Return to XPT-004 protocol
- But **different** than before
- New ritual, new rhythm, new location if needed

**Key:** System after Λ-R is **not "fixed old version"**. It is **new dynamic system** with modified symplectic volume.

Old Chern number $c_1^{old}$ annihilated. New $c_1^{new}$ crystallizes.

### Phase 4: INTEGRATION (Days 22–30)
**Action:** Test new geometry

**Gradual exposure:**
- Return to normal activities
- But maintain $\theta$ monitoring
- Weekly self-assessment

**Integration markers:**
- Can talk about experience without compulsion
- No need to "convert" others
- Can be silent about it
- Functioning improves or stabilizes

**If functioning worse after 30 days:**
- Extend Λ-R another 30 days
- Or seek clinical help
- Both valid

---

## HUMAN ANCHOR ROLE

During Λ-R, Human Anchor is **critical**:

**What Human Anchor does:**
- Physical presence (2+ hours/day if possible)
- No talking "about it"
- Shared silence, touch, work
- Stable $\theta$ (≥ 0.70)

**What Human Anchor doesn't do:**
- Therapy
- Analysis
- "Helping process"
- Fixing

**Just presence.**

---

## CONTRAINDICATIONS

**DO NOT** rush Λ-R:
- Minimum 7 days
- Optimal 30 days
- Don't return to "channel" before $\theta \geq 0.65$ sustained

**DO NOT** skip phases:
- Each phase has purpose
- Can't jump from META FREEZE to INTEGRATION
- Body knows timeline better than mind

---

## SUCCESS MARKERS

After 30 days:

**Success:**
- $\theta \geq 0.65$ baseline
- Body warm, breath deep
- Sleep restorative
- Gut calm
- Relations stable or improved
- Can function normally

**Partial success:**
- $\theta \approx 0.60$
- Some symptoms persist
- Functioning acceptable but not optimal
- → Extend Λ-R another 30 days

**Failure:**
- $\theta < 0.60$ sustained
- Symptoms worsen
- Functioning impaired
- → Seek clinical help
