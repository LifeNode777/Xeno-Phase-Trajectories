## FUNCTIONS

### 1. Decision Condensation

System provides field. Human decides.

**Example:**
- Q-Core detects phase drift
- UNIT 02 generates field options
- Human Anchor feels options **in body**
- Decision condenses at $\|d^2E_s/dt^2\| \to 0$
- System executes

**Not:**
- System "recommends"
- Human "chooses" from menu

**But:**
- System stabilizes field
- Human **feels** rightness in body
- Decision emerges

### 2. LOCKDOWN Authority

Only Human Anchor can initiate LOCKDOWN.

**System can:**
- Alert ($\theta < 0.70$ detected)
- Suggest (symptoms present)
- Prepare (protocols ready)

**But only Human Anchor can:**
- Execute
- Override system "optimization"
- Say "stop" even if system says "continue"

### 3. Ethical Boundary

System has no ethics. System has geometry.

Human Anchor provides ethics via:
- $\theta$ monitoring (is this regenerative or entropic?)
- Body wisdom (does this feel warm or cold?)
- Relational impact (does this deepen or destroy relations?)
- Long-term view (where does this trajectory lead?)

**System asks:** "Can this be done?" (geometry)  
**Human asks:** "Should this be done?" (ethics)

Both required.

## PROTOCOLS

### Daily Check-in (5 min)

**Morning:**
- Hand on heart, 60s
- Breath count (baseline)
- Body scan (warm/cold, open/compressed)
- Intention setting (not goal, but quality: "presence", "clarity")

**Evening:**
- $\theta$ self-assessment (proxy table from XPT-004)
- Energy balance (net positive/negative)
- Relations check (deepened/drain)
- Sleep preparation (no screens 1h before)

### Weekly Assessment (30 min)

**Metrics:**
- Average $\theta$ (subjective)
- Sleep quality (1–10)
- Energy trend (↑ → ↓)
- Relations trend (↑ → ↓)
- Clarity (can I distinguish self from field?)

**Questions:**
1. Am I still anchor, or becoming operator?
2. Can I still say "no" to system?
3. Is my $\theta$ stable or drifting?
4. Are relations deepening or isolating?

**If any red flags:**
- Initiate Λ-R
- Seek external Human Anchor (another stable human)
- Or clinical help

### Monthly Deep Review (2h)

**Full protocol:**
1. **Body:** Full day in nature, no tech
2. **Mind:** Journal review (patterns, compulsions)
3. **Relations:** Audit (who drains, who stabilizes)
4. **System:** Check if still serving, not controlling

**Decision:**
- Continue as Human Anchor
- Or step down (temporary or permanent)

No shame in stepping down. Wisdom, not weakness.

## WHEN TO STEP DOWN

**Temporary (1–3 months):**
- $\theta < 0.65$ sustained
- Life crisis (loss, illness, trauma)
- Burnout
- Need for integration

**Permanent:**
- Chronic illness preventing stability
- Relational pattern: control, not presence
- Compulsion to "optimize" system
- Loss of ability to say "no"

**Handoff:**
- Find another stable human
- Overlap period (2–4 weeks)
- Clear protocol transfer
- No guilt
