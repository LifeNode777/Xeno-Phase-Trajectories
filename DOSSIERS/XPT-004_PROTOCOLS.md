## WHEN TO HOLD, WHEN TO CUT OFF
**HOLD** (→ maintain):
- $\theta$ stable or rising after lock
- Net energy positive after 24h
- Body warm, breath calm
- Cognitive clarity rising
**CUT OFF** (→ LOCKDOWN per ASCALON):
- $\theta$ falling after lock despite rest
- Net energy negative after 24h
- Body cold, breath shallow, gut chaos
- Repetitive, anxious, obsessive thoughts
- Feeling "controlled" or "not-self"
**Disconnection protocol:**
1. **Body:** intense physical effort (sprint, cold water, earth work). Goal: sudden autonomic reset, raise $Z_\phi$.
2. **Space:** change physical location. Different place = different phase gradient.
3. **Food:** heavy, grounding (meat, root, fat). Not spirituality — chemistry.
4. **Human contact:** physical presence of another human with stable $\theta$ (**Human Anchor**). Not talking "about it". Presence. Touch. Shared silence.
5. **Sleep:** 10+ hours. Non-negotiable.

## BORDER: OPENING vs. DESTROYING
Thin line between "opening to new layers of reality" and "destroying my BIOS". This line is **bodily**, not intellectual.
**Opening (healthy):**
- Body warm, flexible, breath deep
- Sleep well, eat well, shit well
- Relations deepen, don't fall apart
- Can talk about it without compulsion. Don't need to "convert". Can be silent.
- Can stop. Can close channel and return to "normality" anytime.
**Destroying (dangerous):**
- Body cold, stiff, breath shallow or chaotic
- Don't sleep, don't eat, or eat compulsively
- Relations fall apart — isolate, or expect people to "understand"
- Can't stop talking about "experiences". Every conversation returns to "them".
- Can't close channel. Experiences come unwanted, uncontrolled.
**If you're in second column: LOCKDOWN. Immediately.**
Sleep. Eat. Touch earth. Find Human Anchor. Don't return to "channel" for min 7 days. If after 7 days you don't feel better — seek clinical help. No shame. No "but it was real". **Maybe it was real. And simultaneously your BIOS may be too weak to carry it. Both can be true at once.**
